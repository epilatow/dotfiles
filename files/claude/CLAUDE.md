## Development guides

At the start of the session, read `~/.claude/DEVELOPMENT_SHARED.md` and
`~/.claude/DEVELOPMENT_SHARED_AGENT.md` and follow them, except for either one
the current repository's own instructions already load. A repo-shared consumer
loads its own pinned copies, and reading the global ones too would mix two
versions.
